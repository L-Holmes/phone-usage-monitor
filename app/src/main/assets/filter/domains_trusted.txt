# TRUSTED DOMAINS - never blocked, by anything, in any mode.
#
# This list OUTRANKS every block we have, including the ~550k downloaded adult blocklist.
# That is deliberate and it is the point of the file: those community lists are built by
# automated categorisation, and automated categorisation is measurably bad at telling a
# sexual-health charity apart from a porn site.
#
# The research is not ambiguous. Filtering studies have found roughly 9% of sexual-health
# sites, 20% of safe-sex sites and 27% of sites about condoms blocked at the LEAST
# aggressive settings. When the UK rolled out ISP-level adult filters, the casualties
# included rape and sexual abuse help centres, domestic violence services, sex education
# charities and - with some irony for us - PORN ADDICTION RECOVERY SITES.
#
# Blocking someone from a rape crisis line, a suicide helpline or an LGBT+ support service
# is not an acceptable cost of blocking pornography. It is a worse outcome than the thing
# we are trying to prevent. So those categories are enumerated here explicitly rather than
# left to chance.
#
# A bare domain also covers its subdomains. One host per line, "#" = comment.
# LANGUAGE-NEUTRAL. Loaded by FilterData -> AppConfig.SAFE_DOMAINS.
#
# ADDING TO THIS FILE IS A SERIOUS ACT: an entry here can never be blocked by anything.
# Add organisations, not platforms. A charity's own site, yes. A site that merely HOSTS
# helpful material alongside everything else, no.
